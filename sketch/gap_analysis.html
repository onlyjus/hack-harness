NETL Directive 402.1-B

Technical Information Management & Security

Effective Date: October 24, 2023

1. PURPOSE

This directive establishes the mandatory requirements for the management, protection, and dissemination of technical information at the National Energy Technology Laboratory (NETL). It ensures that all scientific and technical results are captured, managed, and made available in accordance with Department of Energy (DOE) statutory requirements.

2. SCOPE

This directive applies to all NETL federal and contractor employees engaged in research and development activities, regardless of the funding source or performance location. Current draft lacks specific sub-clause for cross-laboratory collaborative projects.

3. REQUIREMENTS

3.1. Technical reports must be submitted through the Lab's internal clearance process before any public release.

3.2. All data sets must include standardized metadata as defined in the Lab’s Data Management Plan (DMP).

3.3. Researchers must utilize the approved institutional repository for all archival storage of final research data.
