DOE Order 413.3B

Program and Project Management for the Acquisition of Capital Assets

1.0 Scope

This Order applies to all Department of Energy (DOE) elements, including the National Nuclear Security Administration (NNSA), for capital asset projects having a Total Project Cost (TPC) greater than or equal to $50M.

1

2.0 Requirements

Establish a Performance Baseline (PB) prior to Critical Decision (CD-2), "Approve Performance Baseline." The PB is the collective key performance parameters, including TPC, schedule, and scope.

3.0 Exemptions

Projects with TPC less than $50M are exempt from the formal CD process but must still follow established laboratory project management guidelines as specified by institutional policy.

NETL Directive Last Modified: Oct 2023

NETL-D-413.3-01

Project Management Implementation for Capital Assets

Section I: Purpose

Defines NETL-specific implementation of DOE O 413.3B for all capital asset projects within the Lab's purview. Aligns with TPC thresholds of $50M+ for formal reporting.

1

Section II: Baseline Controls

Performance Baselines must be established no later than CD-3. Minor projects ($20M-$50M) may utilize a "Modified Baseline" structure pending Laboratory Director approval.
